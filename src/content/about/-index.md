I am Jiyu Yan, a software engineer based in the UK. I like solving practical problems, building useful tools, and making complicated systems easier to work with.

I spent most of my life in China and moved to the UK for postgraduate study. I have now been here for more than six years, which is basically all of my working life as a software engineer.

Most of my work has been in enterprise software: integrations, automation, reporting, backend services, and small tools that remove friction from daily work. I enjoy the part where a messy requirement becomes something clear enough to build, test, and maintain.

Outside work, I fence. It suits me for the same reason engineering does: you have to observe carefully, make decisions under pressure, and keep improving after every mistake.
